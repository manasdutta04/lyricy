# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make taking part in Lyricy a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including toward people who are new to open source
- Respecting different viewpoints, languages and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take appropriate and fair action in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests and discussions) and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/manasdutta04). All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction:** for inappropriate language or other unprofessional behaviour, a private written warning explaining the problem. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning:** for a violation through a single incident or a series of actions, a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people involved for a period of time.
3. **Temporary ban:** for a serious violation or sustained inappropriate behaviour, a temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban:** for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.

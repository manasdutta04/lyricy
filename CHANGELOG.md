## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-13

First public release.

### Added

- Time-synced lyrics in the browser's side panel, with Apple Music-style word-by-word highlighting, blur, and breathing dots during instrumental breaks.
- Works on YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify, SoundCloud, Apple Music, Deezer, Tidal, Amazon Music, JioSaavn, Gaana, Wynk, Pandora, Bandcamp and Audiomack, plus any site via the optional **Any website** setting.
- Lyrics from LRCLIB, NetEase Cloud Music, JioSaavn and lyrics.ovh, with song matching through iTunes Search.
- When no lyrics site has a song: lyrics from the video description, or the video's own captions, synced to the singing.
- **Find it anyway** for a deeper search when nothing is found.
- Lyrics re-timed to each music video using YouTube's auto-captions (intros, live cuts and edits).
- Jukebox and medley videos: each chapter gets its own lyrics.
- Transliteration into English letters for Hindi, Bengali, Punjabi, Gujarati, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and more, with the original script shown alongside.
- Floating lyrics window for fullscreen video, and support for the YouTube mini player, Shorts and background tabs.
